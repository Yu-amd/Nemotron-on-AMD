## Theoretical Nemotron notes

- Nano BF16 (~60 GB): more HBM headroom than MI300X; still needs kernels. **NOT YET VALIDATED.**
- Super BF16 (~240 GB): 256 − 240 = 16 GB leftover — **1× tight**. Long context not assumed. **NOT YET VALIDATED.** Do not write “doesn't fit” on OAM.
- Ultra: still a multi-GPU problem.

Do not copy MI300X results onto MI325X without a run. Same ISA family reduces *some* kernel risk relative to MI350, but memory and firmware still differ.
