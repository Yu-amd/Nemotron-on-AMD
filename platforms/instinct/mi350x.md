## Theoretical Nemotron notes

- **OCP FP8** is a closer conceptual match to NVIDIA FP8 checkpoints than MI300 FNUZ — still **not** a PASS.
- **MXFP4 is not NVFP4.** Do not market MI350 MXFP4 as running NVIDIA NVFP4 checkpoints.
- Super BF16 (~240 GB) has more headroom here than on MI300X (288 − 240). Still needs LatentMoE + MTP + hybrid Mamba kernels on gfx950. **NOT YET VALIDATED.**
- Ultra NVFP4 payload ~275 GB plus overhead: still harsh on one GPU even before format issues.

vLLM docs (2026-08-15) list MI350 (gfx950) with ROCm 7.0+. That is **vLLM platform support**, not Nemotron support.
